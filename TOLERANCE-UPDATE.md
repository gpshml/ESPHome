## 🔍 Why 10mm Tolerance?

The 10mm tolerance provides:

1. **Easy Mattress Installation**
   - Mattress can be easily placed and removed
   - No forcing required

2. **Bedding Management**
   - Fitted sheets can be tucked properly
   - Blankets don't get caught

3. **Construction Tolerances**
   - Accounts for slight imperfections
   - Easier to build accurately

4. **Material Expansion**
   - Wood expands/contracts with humidity
   - Metal frame provides stable reference

5. **Comfort**
   - Mattress not compressed or forced into tight space

## 💡 Can the Tolerance Be Adjusted?

**YES!** If you want different tolerance:

**To change tolerance:**
1. Edit `generate_cabin_bed_drawings.py`
2. Change `BED_TOLERANCE = 10` to your desired value
3. Run: `python3 generate_cabin_bed_drawings.py`
4. New drawings generated automatically

**Common tolerance options:**
- **5mm**: Tighter fit, less clearance
- **10mm**: Recommended (current setting)
- **15mm**: More generous, easier construction
- **20mm**: Very loose, easier bedding but may look gappy

## 📞 Questions?

### "Is 10mm enough clearance?"
**Yes** - Standard for fitted furniture. Mattress fits easily, bedding tucks properly.

### "Will the mattress move around?"
**No** - 10mm per side is minimal. Mattress weight prevents sliding. Can add non-slip mat if concerned.

### "Can I use a different mattress size?"
**Yes** - Adjust `MATTRESS_WIDTH` and `MATTRESS_DEPTH` in Python script, keep tolerance at 10mm.

### "Do drawers need updating?"
**No** - Drawer dimensions unchanged (880 × 900 × 150mm). They fit in the voids as before.

### "Will this fit through my door?"
Construction is in-place, not pre-built. Materials (plasterboard sheets, plywood) are what needs door clearance.
