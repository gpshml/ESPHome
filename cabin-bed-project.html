Phase Task Key Points
1 Floor Preparation Mark footprint, install wooden plank base (173mm high), ensure level, add middle support legs at 940mm positions
2 Left Wall Extension Cut existing wall 100-200mm, extend frame 237mm, re-plasterboard
3 Metal Stud Frame Install floor/head tracks, vertical studs at 400mm centers, noggins at 1150mm (shelf) and 975mm (tablet)
4 Services Route tablet power cable, consider lighting, future-proof with conduit
5 Plasterboard Fix 12.5mm plasterboard, cut access opening (1300 × 580mm), tape/joint, sand, prime, paint
6 Bed Frame Build 357mm high frame around platform edge with 18mm plywood
7 Shelving Cut and install rear shelf (2246 × 210mm) and head shelf (346 × 900mm), flush-mount, finish

9 Tablet Mount Install bracket at center, 975mm height, connect power, cable management

🛡️ Safety Features

  • All sharp corners and edges should be rounded or protected with edge guards
  • Access opening perimeter must have smooth, finished edges
  • Drawer runners must have stops to prevent complete removal
  • Tablet mount must be securely fixed to noggins (not just plasterboard)

  • Ensure adequate ventilation within enclosed space
  • Check weight capacity of shelves for intended use
  • Maintain clear access to electrical outlets
