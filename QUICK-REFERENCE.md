## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. ❌ Not checking level at every stage
2. ❌ Forgetting to route cables before plasterboard
3. ❌ Cutting access opening before plasterboard is up
4. ❌ Not allowing for plasterboard thickness (12.5mm)
5. ❌ Installing drawer runners without testing first
6. ❌ Forgetting noggins for tablet mount
7. ❌ Not leaving expansion gaps for drawers (5mm each side)
8. ❌ Mounting tablet directly to plasterboard (use noggins!)

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Mark Everything** - Use pencil to mark all stud positions
2. **Test Fit First** - Dry-fit components before fixing
3. **Work from Back to Front** - Build rear wall first
4. **Check Level Constantly** - Every 30 minutes during frame build
5. **Pre-drill** - Especially near plywood edges
6. **Take Photos** - Document before covering with plasterboard
7. **Buy Extra Screws** - You'll drop more than you think
8. **Label Cables** - Before they disappear into walls
9. **Clean As You Go** - Plasterboard dust gets everywhere
10. **Don't Rush Jointing** - Better to wait for proper drying

---

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

**"My measurements don't match the plans"**
→ Check you're measuring to the same reference points (internal vs external)

**"Plasterboard won't line up"**
→ Verify stud spacing is 400mm, check for twisted studs

**"Drawers are too tight"**
→ Should have 5mm clearance each side - check drawer widths

**"Tablet mount feels loose"**
→ Must be fixed to noggins, not just plasterboard

**"Access opening is hard to cut"**
→ Use template, drill corner holes first, then jigsaw

**"Shelves aren't level"**
→ Check stud wall tops are level before adding shelf boards

---

## 📞 When to Call a Professional

Consider professional help if:
- Existing wall is load-bearing
- Major electrical work needed
- Unsure about structural capacity
- Floor is uneven by more than 10mm
- Dealing with concrete floors and no experience with anchors
