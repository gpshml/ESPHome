🔧 Key Construction Details

Wall Extension Work

  • Location: Front of foot wall (LEFT side when looking at top plan)
  • Amount: 237mm extending into room
  • Method: Remove plasterboard ~200mm back from current end, extend metal stud frame forward 237mm, re-plasterboard
  • Result: Full 1242mm depth alcove

Coordinate System

  • Origin (0,0,0): Back-left-floor corner where back wall meets foot wall at floor level
  • X-axis: 0 to 2344mm (foot wall to head wall, left to right)
  • Y-axis: 0 to 1242mm (back to front, extending INTO ROOM)
  • Z-axis: 0 to 2380mm (floor to ceiling)

Structure

  • Metal stud frame: 70mm C-studs at 400mm centers
  • Plasterboard: 12.5mm on visible faces
  • Rear wall frame: Stops at 1200mm (shelf level)
  • Front/side frames: Continue to full 2380mm
  • 6 support legs: 20×20mm at corners and midpoints

Electrical & Safety

  • 7" ESP32 touchscreen at 975mm height (rear shelf center)
  • Floor: Timber joists with cables below - max 25-30mm screw depth
  • Primary fixing: Grab adhesive + short screws
  • Always use cable detector before drilling
