Derived from locked dimensions (LOCKED-IN-SPEC.md / cabin_bed_model.js).

Safety checklist

  • ☐ Cable-detect timber floor before any screws
  • ☐ Screws into floor ≤25–30mm
  • ☐ Shelf ledgers fixed into sound existing walls (find structure; don’t rely on plasterboard alone)
  • ☐ Risers 170mm under 360mm bed → platform 530mm AFF
  • ☐ Mains work by qualified electrician
  • ☐ Opening edges finished

Key Construction Details

Wall Extension Work

  • Location: Front of foot wall (LEFT on top plan)
  • Amount: 237mm extending into room
  • Method: Strip plasterboard ~200mm back from current end, extend metal stud frame forward 237mm, re-board

Coordinate System

  • Origin (0,0,0): Back-left-floor corner
  • X: 0 → 2344mm (foot → head)
  • Y: 0 → 1242mm (back → front into room)
  • Z: 0 → 2380mm (floor → ceiling)

Electrical & Safety

  • 7" ESP32 touchscreen at ~975mm AFF (rear shelf centre)
  • Floor: timber joists with cables — max 25–30mm screw depth
  • Primary fixing: grab adhesive + short screws; cable-detect before drilling
