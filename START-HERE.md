## ⚠️ CRITICAL: Floor Installation Safety

**ELECTRICAL CABLES RUN UNDER FLOOR!**

### Do NOT:
- ❌ Use long screws (no longer than 25-30mm!)
- ❌ Drill without checking for cables first
- ❌ Force screws if you feel resistance

### DO:
- ✅ Use cable detector before ANY screw/drill
- ✅ Use construction adhesive + short screws
- ✅ Consider surface-mounted brackets (no floor penetration)
- ✅ Pilot holes for all screws
- ✅ See "Floor Fixing Strategy" in UPDATED-SPECIFICATIONS.md

## 💡 Lighting System Highlights

### Three Systems Designed:

**1. Ambient Warm Lighting**
- LED strips under rear and head shelves
- Warm white (2700K-3000K)
- Dimmable 0-100%
- Creates cozy atmosphere
- Cost: £40-60

**2. Reading Light**
- Adjustable gooseneck or swing-arm
- 4000K neutral white (reduces eye strain)
- Dimmable, 300-500 lumens
- Mounted on head shelf
- Cost: £15-50

**3. Star Projection System** (Choose one):

**Option A: Laser Star Projector** (Easiest)
- Commercial unit (BlissLights or similar)
- Plug and play
- ESP32 controls on/off via smart plug
- Cost: £20-50

**Option B: Fiber Optic Stars** (Most Realistic) ⭐ **RECOMMENDED**
- 200-400 individual fiber strands through ceiling
- RGB LED light engine
- Looks like real embedded stars
- Twinkling effects
- Cost: £50-100

**Option C: LED Matrix** (Most Customizable)
- WS2812B individually addressable LEDs
- Custom animations (twinkling, shooting stars, nebula)
- Music reactive
- Fully programmable
- Cost: £30-60

**Option D: Hybrid** (Best Overall)
- Fiber optic stars + LED nebula effect
- Most realistic and versatile
- Cost: £70-120

### Control:
- Single ESP32-S3 controls all lighting
- 7" touchscreen for scenes and settings
- Mobile app control (WLED)
- Voice control optional (Home Assistant)
- Physical touch buttons on shelf

### Lighting Scenes:
- Bedtime (warm, dim, slow fade)
- Reading (bright, focused)
- Wake Up (sunrise simulation)
- Relax (slow color cycle)
- Party (fast, music reactive)
- Space (dim blue + twinkling stars)
