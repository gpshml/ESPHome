## 🔒 Safety Reminders

- **Structural:** This is a sleeping platform - all connections must be secure
- **Edges:** Round all sharp corners, especially around access opening
- **Tablet mount:** Must fix to noggins (horizontal braces), not just plasterboard
- **Drawers:** Install with stops so they can't be pulled completely out
- **Ventilation:** Ensure adequate air circulation in enclosed space
- **Weight limits:** Shelves designed for books/toys, not adult weight
- **Fire safety:** Keep power cables properly managed and accessible
- **Testing:** Load test platform before daughter uses it
