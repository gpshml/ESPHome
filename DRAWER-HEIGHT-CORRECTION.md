## 🔨 Construction Notes

### Floor-Mounted Runners:
**Previous spec (150mm drawer):**
- Light-duty runners
- 20kg rating would suffice

**Updated spec (500mm drawer):**
- **Heavy-duty runners required!**
- Minimum 30kg rating (preferably 40kg)
- Full extension preferred (900mm travel)
- Ball-bearing type recommended
- Mount directly to floor with robust fixings

### Drawer Construction:
**With 500mm height:**
- Use 12mm plywood for sides (not 6mm)
- Reinforce corners with blocks
- Consider dado joints for strength
- Multiple horizontal bracing recommended
- Ensure base is well-supported

### Materials Adjustment:
**Previous (150mm):**
- Front: 880 × 150mm
- Sides: 900 × 130mm

**Corrected (500mm):**
- Front: 880 × **500mm**
- Sides: 900 × **480mm** (internal height)
- **Significantly more material required!**
